What owners ask us

Won't my customers hate talking to a robot?

Set it up as overflow only - it picks up the calls you're already missing, and the alternative was your voicemail. It introduces itself honestly as an assistant, and any caller can say "get me a human" and be transferred to your cell. You just heard the voice yourself when you called the demo - that's what your callers get instead of a beep.

I already have an answering service.

Most charge $1-2 per minute to take a message. This is a flat rate, books the job directly into your calendar instead of leaving you a note, and never puts anyone on hold. Run both for 30 days and compare the two reports - and the two bills.

What if it breaks or says something wrong?

Conditional forwarding means your existing line is untouched - rollback takes 10 minutes and we document the deactivation code for you on day one. Every call is transcribed, I review transcripts daily during launch week, and I test-call every live receptionist weekly. And you have my cell.

Is this legal in California? Recording, AI disclosure?

Yes - because we build it in. Every receptionist we deploy announces itself as an AI assistant and gives the CIPA (California Invasion of Privacy Act) recording notice up front. California is strict here, and most vendors ignore it. We treat compliance as a feature, not a footnote.

How fast can I be live?

Five business days from kickoff call to launch, usually faster. Soft launch on after-hours first, then full overflow once the transcripts are clean.
